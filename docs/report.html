Harness Bench Preliminary Report
Engineering Test Bench · terminal-bench 2.0 · 27 tasks

Which agent harness gets the most out of each model?

PRELIMINARY · 2% of trials pending rerun for final results
  • Ten agent harnesses ran the same 27 IT, e-commerce and SaaS tasks on six models.
  • Every ratio divides by a reference on the same tasks: 0.80× means 20% less than the reference.
  • The overall reference is Claude Code on Opus 5.5 medium.
  • Harnesses are compared with Claude Code on Claude models and with Goose on the other models, at the same model and effort.
  • Models are compared with the same harness on Opus 5.5 medium.

Executive insights

  • Model choice correlates more with task success; harness choice correlates more with cost.On Claude models every harness passed 25–27 of 27 tasks, a spread too small to tell apart at this sample size. Cost varied widely: at Opus medium, Pi cost 31% less than Claude Code, Goose 21% less, and Cline 60% more.
  • Sonnet 5.5 stood out among the mid-tier models on these terminal-bench tasks.Across the 8 harnesses that ran every model, Sonnet passed 209 of 216 (97%), against 92–94% for Kimi K3, GPT-6.1 Terra and DeepSeek, 89% for Grok and 80% for GLM. It was also the fastest (1.6 min median per task, against 2.1–5.6) and tied with Terra as the cheapest (2.6¢). The pass gap to the next models is a few tasks; the cost and speed gaps are larger. For scale, Opus medium passed 211 of 216 at about three times Sonnet's cost.
  • Claude Code was the fastest harness on Claude models, not the cheapest.The other harnesses took 1.3–2.2× as long as Claude Code at Opus medium. Where people wait on the agent, that speed may justify its cost; for unattended batch work, Pi and Goose reached the same pass counts for less.
  • Open models cost less, and each tended to do best on a particular harness.The top harness differed by model: Kimi K3 on Pi (27 of 27, among the cheapest and fastest), DeepSeek V4.1 Flash on OpenCode or Terminus-2 (26 of 27 at the lowest cost), GLM 5.3 on Cline (24 of 27). GLM had the widest spread across harnesses (20 to 24). Most of these margins are one to four tasks, so treat each pairing as a starting point to confirm, not a settled ranking.
  • Grok 4.7 was the most expensive and slowest option in this test.On Goose it cost 1.9× Opus medium and took 3.3× as long, with 25 vs 27 passes. Some of the slowness coincided with an overloaded test machine and a rerun is planned; the cost gap is less likely to change.
  • Some agents with internet access looked up the benchmark's answers.In 11 runs, mostly on DeepSeek, the agent downloaded the benchmark's own answer key and used it; we count those as fails. Published scores for internet-connected agents may be inflated, and the same behaviour in production means fetching and running unvetted code, so internet access warrants a deliberate policy.

⭐ Worth looking into: no fewer passes than its reference, cost and time within 5% or better (1.05× or less), and at least 5% better on one of them (0.95× or less). Differences under 5% count as a tie.

Why this is preliminary

Checked

Prices

Every rate used here matches the providers' price pages as of 3 Nov 2026. Costs are token counts × those rates. Long-context price tiers are not applied, so very long requests are priced slightly low.

Checked

Kilo and OpenCode on Opus

Their Opus high and medium trials often reused a prompt cache written minutes earlier by another trial, which makes them look cheaper than Opus low. This is real cache behaviour, not a counting bug; costed as if every trial started cold, high and medium would be 1.4–1.9× low.

Checked

Cost records

Every trial with token counts is costed, DeepSeek Harness included. The report recomputes cost from tokens rather than trusting each harness's own figure.

Settled

Our own faults and answer lookups

Trials lost to our setup were excluded and rerun; provider stalls count as fails. Agents that fetch the benchmark's own answer files are flagged automatically, confirmed by hand and counted as fails. Fetching a real upstream fix, such as a CVE patch, is allowed.

Rerun pending

37 trials (2%) being rerun

Trials that may have failed for reasons on our side are being rerun for the final results: 3 where the harness install could not reach github.com, 14 fails while the test machine's CPU was busy, and 20 timeouts from a period with no CPU record. Ten passes from the same period are rerun alongside as a control.

Method. terminal-bench 2.0 on Harbor 0.25.0, one attempt per task. Open models (DeepSeek, GLM, Kimi) run through Fireworks at high effort; Terra and Grok at medium. Claude Code runs only on Claude models, so on the other models Goose is the reference harness. DeepSeek Harness runs only on DeepSeek, so it has no model comparison. A pass is the task's own tests passing. Medians are per trial.
